Key timing trick (SVS & HoC): For troop training, points are awarded when training finishes, not when it starts. Queue large batches 1–2 days early so completion falls on the Combat Training / Hero Dev stage. Similarly, queue expensive building upgrades the day before Construction day — they start awarding points the moment the stage resets.
